// about

A developer's toolkit, a trainer's patience.

I'm Bhavesh Mathur, a web developer and technical trainer from Nagpur. I completed my graduation at Dharampeth College of Science, Nagpur, and since then I've split my time between two things I genuinely enjoy: building websites for clients as a freelancer, and helping students understand how the web actually works.

As a freelancer, I've worked on projects that needed clean, functional front-to-back solutions delivered on real deadlines. As a trainer, I break those same concepts down for students who are just starting out — because knowing how to build something and knowing how to teach it are two very different skills, and I've worked on both.
